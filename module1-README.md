## Module Summary

A Security Operations Centre (SOC) is the team responsible for monitoring, detecting, and responding to security threats inside an organization. The SOC runs 24/7 and is structured in tiers — each with a different level of responsibility.

As a Tier 1 analyst (the role this path prepares you for), the job is to monitor incoming alerts, triage them to determine if they are real threats or false positives, and escalate confirmed incidents to Tier 2 for deeper investigation.

Attackers target organizations through two main channels — people (social engineering) and systems (technical vulnerabilities). Understanding both is essential before diving into tools and detection.
