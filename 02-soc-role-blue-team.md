# Room 2 — SOC Role in Blue Team

## What This Room Covers
Where the SOC sits within the broader security organization, and the fundamental difference between Red Team and Blue Team operations.

---

## Red Team vs Blue Team

| | Red Team | Blue Team |
|---|----------|-----------|
| **Goal** | Attack and find weaknesses | Defend and detect attacks |
| **Mindset** | Think like an attacker | Think like a defender |
| **Activities** | Penetration testing, phishing simulations, exploit development | Monitoring, alert triage, incident response |
| **Output** | Vulnerability reports | Incident reports, detection improvements |

> The SOC is the core of the Blue Team. While Red Teams simulate attacks, the SOC is what defends against real ones happening right now.

---

## What the SOC Monitors

- Network traffic for suspicious patterns
- Endpoint activity (logins, process execution, file changes)
- Log data from servers, firewalls, and applications
- Security alerts generated by tools like SIEM and EDR

---

## Key Terms

| Term | Definition |
|------|------------|
| Blue Team | The defensive security team responsible for protecting the organization |
| Red Team | The offensive security team that simulates attacks to find weaknesses |
| Purple Team | A collaboration between Red and Blue — Red attacks, Blue detects, both improve together |
| SOC | The operational heart of the Blue Team |

---

## My Takeaway
Red and Blue are not opposites — they serve the same goal from different angles. As someone also doing VAPT work, understanding both sides makes me a stronger analyst. Knowing how attackers think directly improves how I detect them.
